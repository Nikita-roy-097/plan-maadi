How Plan Maadi works

Every listing links to the page where you book it.

Prices are the lowest ticket shown on the listing. Organisers change times and sell out, so check the listing before you go. Distances are measured along real roads, using the quickest usual route by car, and drive times follow Bengaluru's usual traffic for the hour you're travelling (rush hours from TomTom's 2025 Traffic Index). Tap Directions for live traffic in Google Maps. Constraint details (smoking areas, pets, step-free access, noise) come from venue listings and reviews; when a venue doesn't say, the plan flags it so you can call ahead. BookMyShow and most Instagram pages can't be read automatically yet, so their events only appear when they're also listed elsewhere.

Road distances are worked out from road data © OpenStreetMap contributors and TomTom, via Overture Maps, under the ODbL. Map tiles © OpenStreetMap contributors; routes drawn with OSRM.
